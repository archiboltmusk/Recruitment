What is 20% NPA?

Non-Practicing Allowance (20% of Basic Pay) is paid in Central Govt (CHS, Railways, ESIC, AIIMS) in exchange for zero private practice. DA is also calculated on Basic + NPA.

PG Bond & NEET PG

Contractual walk-in roles (Railways CMP, MCD, Non-Acad JR, ECHS) have zero bond, allowing you to resign with 15–30 days notice when you crack NEET PG or UPSC CMS.

State SMC NOC Rule

Central Govt (UPSC CMS / AIIMS / Railways) accepts any State Medical Council or NMC registration. State PSCs & state NHMs generally require local State Council registration/NOC at joining.
